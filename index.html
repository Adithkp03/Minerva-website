01 — About

A live band forged on the competition circuit. We play what the room needs, at full volume.

Formed as a college band in 2023, Minerva brings together a unique blend of creativity, musical expression and live energy. Through hard work and a shared commitment to their music, they have established themselves on Pune's Battle of Bands circuit, performing across prestigious institutions and earning championship titles and podium finishes along the way.

The winning commitment shapes their approach to every stage, bringing rock, pop, Bollywood and crowd favourites into sets built around the occasion and the audience.
